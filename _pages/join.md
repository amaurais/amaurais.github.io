My research group is launching at Georgia Tech in Fall 2027! We will develop computational methods for probabilistic inference with a focus on how geometry and structure enable effective design and efficient computation. We will also collaborate with domain scientists and engineers to bring advanced methods for Bayesian inference, uncertainty quantification, generative modeling, and data science to their problems. 

I anticipate hiring 1-2 PhD students to join me at Georgia Tech starting in Fall 2027. Ideal candidates will have strong backgrounds in applied or computational mathematics, statistics, computer science, or engineering and be proficient in at least one programming language. If you would like to be considered for one of these positions, please apply to the [Computational Science &amp Engineering](https://www.cc.gatech.edu/degree-programs/phd-computational-science-and-engineering) or [Machine Learning](https://ml.gatech.edu/phd-program) PhD Program at Georgia Tech with CSE as your home unit and mention my name in your application. 

I highly encourage prospective PhD students to apply for graduate fellowships (e.g., the [NSF GRFP](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/initiatives/grfp), [DOE CSGF](https://www.krellinst.org/csgf/), [NDSEG Fellowship](https://ndseg.sysplus.com/), etc.). If you would like to collaborate on a fellowship application or need a faculty sponsor for an application, please feel free to email me (and please be cognizant of relevant deadlines!). If you email me, please attach your CV and describe your research interests, why you would like to work with me, and why you are pursuing a PhD.
